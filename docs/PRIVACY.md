# Sahayak (सहायक) — Privacy Policy & Security Declaration

> **Effective Date**: November 2026  
> **Extension Architecture**: Manifest V3 Local-First  
> **Privacy Philosophy**: Zero Telemetry • Local Credentials • User-Approved Actions

---

## 1. Zero Telemetry & Tracking Declaration

Sahayak is built on a strict privacy-first foundation:

- **No Analytics**: Sahayak contains no Google Analytics, Segment, Mixpanel, or third-party telemetry beacons.
- **No Remote Logging**: Your queries, summaries, reading history, and browser tasks are never transmitted to any logging server operated by Sahayak.
- **No Background Surveillance**: Sahayak does not passively monitor, track, or record your browsing habits. Webpage extraction occurs only when you deliberately open a feature or request assistance.

---

## 2. API Credentials & Local Storage

- **Local Storage Exclusively**: Your Gemini API key and Groq API key are stored locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`.
- **No Intermediate Proxies**: When making AI requests, your browser connects directly to Google AI Studio (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`) or Groq (`api.groq.com`). Keys are never sent to proxy servers or relays.
- **Local Profile Memory**: All learned user preferences, persona data, and personal memory records are saved solely on your local device. You retain full authority to view, edit, or delete any record at any time.

---

## 3. Data Transmitted to AI Providers

When you choose to trigger an AI-assisted feature, the following data is transmitted directly over encrypted HTTPS to your selected AI provider (Google Gemini or Groq):

| Feature Invocation         | Data Transmitted                                                                                       |
| :------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Article Summarization**  | Extracted readability article text (ads, navigation, and comments stripped).                           |
| **YouTube Summarization**  | Public video TimedText transcript and video title/description.                                         |
| **Grounded Page Chat**     | Relevant text chunks retrieved via local TF-IDF, plus your active question.                            |
| **Screenshot-and-Ask**     | Downscaled JPEG/PNG viewport capture of your active browser tab.                                       |
| **Find It For Me / Agent** | Anonymized element candidate labels (e.g. "Submit Ticket", "Search"), tag names, and your instruction. |

> **Important Disclosure**: Because requests are processed by Google AI Studio or Groq, your interaction with those services is subject to their respective terms of service and privacy policies. Sahayak does not intercept or retain copies of these transmissions outside your local browser.

---

## 4. Deterministic Safety Boundaries

To prevent accidental data exposure or unauthorized transactions, Sahayak's TypeScript code strictly enforces deterministic blocks against:

- Interacting with `password`, `otp`, `cvv`, `pin`, or sensitive credential input fields.
- Submitting forms or triggering financial checkout procedures.
- Navigating to unverified protocols (`javascript:`, `data:`, `file:`, `chrome:`).
- Loading URLs containing embedded user credentials (`https://user:pass@host`).

---

## 5. Your Rights & Data Controls

You have complete control over all data generated by Sahayak:

1. **Export Skills & Settings**: Export reusable workflows to portable JSON without credentials or personal data.
2. **Clear Action Logs**: Wipe session audit logs and execution histories with one click.
3. **Reset Memory**: Purge all local profile memories and preference taxonomies instantly from the Memory tab.
4. **Remove Keys**: Clear all stored API credentials in the Settings tab.
