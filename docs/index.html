Philosophy & Architecture

Human-In-The-Loop By Design

Autonomous AI agents that execute actions without asking can delete data or trigger unwanted transactions. Sahayak puts you in command at every step.

01
👁️

1. Understand

Sahayak analyzes the active webpage, extracting article readability text, YouTube transcripts, or interactive DOM candidate elements only when you invoke it.

02
💡

2. Assist

The AI proposes a grounded answer, summary, or plans a single next action (e.g. scrolling or clicking). The action is validated against local safety rules and assigned a deterministic risk level.

03
🔒

3. Stay in Control

You review the proposal on the Side Panel's Approval Card. You can preview in Dry-Run Mode or click Approve. Without your click and a fresh approval token, nothing executes.

Security By Code

Deterministic Safety & Privacy First

Sahayak does not rely on system prompts or LLM promises to remain safe. Code-level rules guarantee your browser is protected.

🔑

Local-Only Credentials

Your Gemini and Groq API keys are stored exclusively in your local browser via chrome.storage.local. They are never sent to intermediate proxy servers or telemetry databases.

📊

Zero Telemetry

No Google Analytics, no session trackers, and no remote usage logging. What you browse, read, and summarize remains strictly on your device.

🌐

Transparent AI Context

When you trigger a summary or question, only the relevant page content or screenshot is sent directly to your configured provider (Google AI Studio or Groq).

Quick Setup

Install Sahayak in Under 2 Minutes

Sahayak is fully open and packaged for direct installation in Google Chrome Developer Mode.

📦 Download sahayak-v1.0.0.zip
Packaged Release Archive • Manifest V3 • Size: 841 KB
1

Extract the Release ZIP

Download the ZIP archive and extract it to a convenient folder on your machine (e.g. C:\Sahayak-v1.0.0 or ~/sahayak).

2

Open Chrome Extensions

In Google Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions in your URL address bar.

3

Enable Developer Mode

Toggle the Developer mode switch located in the upper-right corner of the Extensions page.

4

Click "Load unpacked"

Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left toolbar, and select the extracted extension directory (containing manifest.json).

5

Open Side Panel & Configure API Key

Click the Sahayak icon in your browser toolbar to open the Side Panel. Go to Settings to enter your free Google Gemini API key or Groq API key.
